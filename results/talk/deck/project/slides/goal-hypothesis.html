GOAL · ONE QUESTION, ONE HYPOTHESIS

Hypothesis: a seasonal rain-day-count contract cuts a park’s bad-season losses where Kalshi’s daily contract does not

BASELINE (WHAT EXISTS)

No hedge; or buy Kalshi’s daily rain contract at Trenton. It pays $1 if the day’s total is at least 0.01 inch. Listed about 1.5 days ahead.

SOLUTION (WHAT WE OFFER)

A contract on the season’s count of loss days, in six buckets. Built as an LMSR market on Sepolia with an oracle.

METRIC

Hedge effectiveness on bad seasons: how much of the downside in net cost (loss − payout + premium) the hedge removes. Scored on held-out seasons only.
