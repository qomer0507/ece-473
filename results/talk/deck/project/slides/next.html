NEXT · LIMITATIONS AND WHAT COMES AFTER

A park can hedge rain on a prediction market, but only with a contract shaped like its loss and settled where it stands

WHAT WE CANNOT SAY

  • Prices beyond Kalshi’s few traded months are modelled, not observed
  • The stand-in gauge is not the park; the park’s own basis risk is unmeasured
  • Trenton’s held-out window is 12 seasons; intervals are wide
  • The market has simulated traders and one trusted reporter

WHAT WE WOULD DO NEXT

  • Settle on rain measured at the business (radar-gauge grids), not at an airport
  • Run the market with real traders on testnet over one season
  • Replace the single reporter with a decentralised oracle
  • Test the count contract for construction, which budgets bad-weather days the same way

The market that predicts rain well is, for that very reason, the wrong place to buy rain insurance as it exists today.
